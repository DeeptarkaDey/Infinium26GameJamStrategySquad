# Infinium26GameJamStrategySquad

Mirage is a story-driven adventure game combining exploration, combat, puzzles and mystery. You take the role of a samurai who follows the orders of a monk. Each mission sends the samurai after a supposedly dangerous person. However, strange events, unexplained occurrences and contradictions in the monk's stories gradually make the player question what they are being told.
